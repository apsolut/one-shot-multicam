How it works

1 · TranscribeWord timings, snapped to your script.
2 · PlanInsert shots on phrase boundaries, cameras alternate.
3 · Re-shootEach insert re-films your real take from a new angle (LTX-2.3 + CrossView).
4 · CutCut on word boundaries over your real, untouched audio.
