How it works

1 · Transcribefaster-whisper word timings (snapped to your script).
2 · PlanInsert shots on phrase boundaries, B/C alternation, 5 s generation chunks.
3 · GenerateComfy Cloud: 8 angle stills, then LTX-2.3 ID LoRA speaks each chunk in your voice.
4 · Align + cutWhisper on the generated clips → offset + retime to your words; ffmpeg cuts over the real audio.

Only your own face, or someone who explicitly consented. The labeled export marks AI cameras.
